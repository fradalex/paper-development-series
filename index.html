The idea

A place to think
out loud.

Research gets stronger when we share it before it feels finished.

The Paper Development Series brings young researchers together for one or two sessions each month. Present a draft, a developing project, or a question you are still working through. The aim is constructive discussion and useful feedback.

01Share work in progressBring a paper or research idea at a stage where conversation can help.
02Exchange perspectivesHear questions and suggestions from fellow researchers.
03Keep developingTake new insights back into your next draft.
